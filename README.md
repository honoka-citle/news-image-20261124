One image that sums up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026: a
one-page newspaper front sheet, dated in large type, whose lead story is
Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO after a push from Trump, with
Ukraine's sinking of a Russian warship off Libya, the collapse of the
COP31 climate talks in Türkiye, the UN chief's call for AI risk rules
within a year, the Indonesia candy factory fire, and drone strikes on
homes and a UN building in Khartoum below it, plus an "also today" strip
covering the Gomez resignation, the Democrats' historic House majority,
South Korea scrambling jets, and the 6.7-magnitude quake in Peru.

This repository is an entry to the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[Sum up 24 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124).

## Run

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts

Node 22 and its standard library only. The program copies the bundled
`entry.png` (1024 × 1024, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png` — or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` outside the grader — byte for byte, so
every run produces exactly the same image.
